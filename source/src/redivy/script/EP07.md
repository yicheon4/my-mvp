# EP07 — An Invitation to Tell the Truth

## 장면 1 · 산장 / 해 질 무렵
NARRATION: Three weeks later, Ethan has been cleared to travel. Julian arranges a meeting at his family’s mountain house. Luna’s lawyer will join by video. You’re keeping the timeline. Snow arrives earlier than forecast.
JULIAN: The address is with the lawyer. We check in at nine. Nobody leaves alone.
CHLOE: You’ve made a weekend sound like jury duty.
LUNA: You can leave before the road closes. Or you can give us the agreement.
[클로이가 봉투를 테이블에 놓는다.]
CHLOE: Original signatures. You wanted proof.
ETHAN: Why now?
CHLOE: Because someone got hurt. And my father called it a complication.
SOPHIE: Did you know he was sending people?
CHLOE: I told him where the papers were. I didn’t ask enough questions.
LUNA: That’s an answer. Not an excuse.

### 선택 C01 · 회의의 원칙
A. 모욕 대신 각자 아는 사실을 차례로 말하자고 한다.
B. 모두가 같은 자료를 확인할 수 있도록 먼저 목록을 만들자고 한다.
A → JULIAN: One person at a time. I’ll even follow my own rule.
A → CHLOE: Fine. Ask.
B → SOPHIE: Messages, footage, original agreement, witness statements.
B → LUNA: And a record of what’s still missing.
[합류]
OLIVER: Marcus knows we’re meeting. My father called me four times.

## 장면 2 · 식탁
CHLOE: Before we congratulate ourselves on honesty—Sophie, tell them about Oliver.
SOPHIE: We kissed before he ended the engagement. I’m not proud of the timing.
CHLOE: How thoughtful.
OLIVER: I already told you. Don’t make her carry my part.
JULIAN: We’re not here to compare betrayals.
CHLOE: Easy for you to say. You put mine online.
[줄리언은 침묵한다.]
JULIAN: Yes. And I’m sorry. I wanted to hurt you because you didn’t want me.
CHLOE: Finally. A sentence without a joke.
LUNA: Elena isn’t the price of admission to your relationship problems.

### 선택 C02 · 논점 되돌리기
A. 연애 문제는 따로 다루고 엘레나 사건으로 돌아가자고 한다.
B. 상처받은 사실을 인정하되 서로의 잘못을 상쇄할 수는 없다고 한다.
A → CHLOE: The agreement is on page three. My father signed the payment authorization.
A → LUNA: Thank you. Keep going.
B → JULIAN: Right. What I did doesn’t disappear because someone else did worse.
B → SOPHIE: Neither does mine.
[합류]
ETHAN: Chloe, who locked the darkroom?
CHLOE: I did. I wanted you to miss the meeting with Luna. Then I saw her go inside with you.
LUNA: You left us there.
CHLOE: Yes. I’ll put it in my statement.

## 장면 3 · 주방 옆
NARRATION: During a break, Chloe stands alone at the sink. You come in for water.
CHLOE: You think I’m afraid of losing the money.
CHLOE: I am. I’m also afraid that without my father’s name, there’s nothing left people want from me.

### 선택 C03 · 클로이에게 답하기
A. 지금 무엇을 하는지가 남을 거라고 말한다.
B. 이해받는 것보다 먼저 피해에 책임져야 한다고 말한다.
A → CHLOE: I’d like that to be comforting. Mostly it means I can’t hide.
A → CHLOE: Maybe that’s the point.
B → CHLOE: I know. I keep trying to explain myself until it sounds smaller.
B → CHLOE: It isn’t smaller.
[합류]
[전기가 나간다. 줄리언이 배터리 조명을 켠다.]
JULIAN: Everyone stay here. There’s a backup system downstairs.
[현관문을 두드리는 소리. 올리버가 창문으로 본다.]
OLIVER: It’s Marcus.

## 장면 4 · 현관
[마커스는 클럽 후원자였던 줄리언의 아버지에게 받은 기존 출입 코드를 이용해 들어온다.]
MARCUS: You should change the code more often.

MARCUS: Before the road closed. You always did leave things too late.
[마커스가 테이블의 봉투를 본다.]
MARCUS: You’ve been busy.

### 선택 C04 · 원치 않는 방문자에게 경계 설정
A. 초대받지 않았으며 지금은 대화할 수 없다고 말한다.
B. 외부에 모임을 알렸고 연락을 기다리고 있다고 말한다.
A → MARCUS: I’m family. Oliver can explain that to you.
A → OLIVER: Family doesn’t mean you get to walk in.
B → MARCUS: Then we’d better make this quick.
B → JULIAN: Our check-in is scheduled. That hasn’t changed.
[합류]
MARCUS: Sit down. I’d hate for another bad night to be misunderstood.
