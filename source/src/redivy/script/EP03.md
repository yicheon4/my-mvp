# EP03 — What We Owe the Dead

## 장면 1 · 암실 / 구조를 기다리는 동안
NARRATION: Campus security is on its way. Ethan turns on the work light. In the sudden brightness, Luna looks less angry than exhausted.
LUNA: You had a year to find me.
ETHAN: I didn’t know she had a sister. Your family’s name was kept out of the reports.
LUNA: I use my mother’s surname. Elena used our father’s.
ETHAN: I’m sorry.
LUNA: Sorry for what? Be specific.
ETHAN: For believing the first person who told me to leave it alone.
ETHAN: I saw a man grab her arm. Then I lost sight of them. I heard the fall.
LUNA: Oliver?
ETHAN: I thought so. His coat. His build. I never saw a face.
LUNA: You let a guess sit there for a year.
ETHAN: I know.

### 선택 C01 · 목격 정보 확인하기
A. 직접 본 것과 나중에 들은 것을 구분해 달라고 한다.
B. 처음 진술을 했다는 기록이 남아 있는지 묻는다.
A → ETHAN: I saw the coat. I heard people say Oliver had been upstairs. Those are different things.
A → LUNA: From now on, keep them different.
B → ETHAN: An appointment email. And the draft I wrote that morning.
B → LUNA: Keep both. Not just the version you remember now.
[합류]
ETHAN: Chloe has something else. A signed agreement. Her father’s office paid to keep the roof camera footage out of the internal review.
LUNA: How do you know?
ETHAN: She showed me the envelope when she was drunk. Then put it in her safe.

## 장면 2 · 암실 / 관계의 진실
LUNA: So you’re using her.
ETHAN: I stayed close to get the document.
LUNA: And kissing her was research?
[이든은 즉시 답하지 못한다.]
ETHAN: No. That was selfish. I’m not asking you to make it noble.
LUNA: Good. Because I won’t.
[루나가 의자에 앉는다.]
LUNA: Elena kept calling me that week. I thought she wanted money.
ETHAN: You didn’t know.
LUNA: I keep hearing that. It doesn’t help.

### 선택 C02 · 죄책감에 반응하기
A. 지금 당장 괜찮아질 필요는 없다고 말한다.
B. 엘레나에 대해 기억하고 싶은 이야기를 물어본다.
A → LUNA: Thank you. I’m tired of people trying to finish this feeling for me.
A → ETHAN: We can just sit here.
B → LUNA: She laughed before she finished a joke. You had to wait for the ending.
B → ETHAN: Every time. Even when the joke was terrible.
[합류]
[문이 열린다. 보안 직원의 화면 밖 목소리.]
SECURITY: Everybody okay? We’ll need statements about the lock.
ETHAN: You’ll get them.
[복도에서 루나와 이든이 잠깐 마주 본다.]
LUNA: I don’t trust you yet.
ETHAN: Fair.
LUNA: But don’t disappear.
ETHAN: I won’t.
[이번 회차에서는 키스하지 않는다. 신뢰 회복이 먼저 시작된다.]

## 장면 3 · 조정부 라커룸 앞
NARRATION: The next afternoon, Sophie asks you to wait outside while she checks on Oliver. He leaves the door open.
OLIVER: Did Chloe send you?
SOPHIE: Yes.
OLIVER: At least you said it.
SOPHIE: She wanted me to keep you distracted. I came because you looked awful.
OLIVER: Efficient. Two jobs, one visit.
SOPHIE: I can leave.
OLIVER: Don’t.
OLIVER: My family likes people who make things easy. Chloe looks right in photographs. My brother says I should be grateful.
SOPHIE: For being convenient?
OLIVER: You make it sound worse when you say it normally.
[올리버가 문밖의 사용자에게도 말한다.]
OLIVER: Is it possible to disappoint everyone and still be a decent person?

### 선택 C03 · 자신의 선택을 할 권리
A. 다른 사람을 실망시키는 것과 해치는 것은 다르다고 말한다.
B. 본인이 원하는 것이 무엇인지 먼저 물어본다.
A → OLIVER: I wish someone had told me that sooner.
A → SOPHIE: You can hear it now.
B → OLIVER: One decision that doesn’t arrive with my family’s signature.
B → SOPHIE: Start smaller. Do you want me here?
[합류]
OLIVER: Yes.
SOPHIE: I want to kiss you. That’s not Chloe’s idea.
OLIVER: I know.
[서로 잠깐 확인한 뒤 입맞춤. 사용자는 시선을 돌려 자리를 비켜준다.]

## 장면 4 · 기숙사 복도 / 저녁
NARRATION: Later, Sophie catches up with you. Her phone keeps buzzing.
SOPHIE: I told myself I was helping. Then I kissed someone who’s still engaged.

### 선택 C04 · 친구에게 솔직하게 말하기
A. 감정이 진짜여도 약혼 문제는 먼저 해결해야 한다고 말한다.
B. 올리버에게 관계를 분명하게 해 달라고 요청하라고 한다.
A → SOPHIE: I know. I don’t want to become another excuse.
A → SOPHIE: I’ll tell him.
B → SOPHIE: No secret arrangement. No waiting until it’s convenient.
B → SOPHIE: That sounds terrifying. And fair.
[합류]
[관객 전용: 소피가 클로이의 전화를 받는다.]
CHLOE: Luna’s still here. Change of plan.
SOPHIE: What plan?
CHLOE: She leaves the faculty office with a stolen exam. Security finds it. You make sure she’s there.
SOPHIE: You’re asking me to ruin her life.
CHLOE: I’m asking whether you’d prefer I ruin yours.
END CARD: One honest answer. One impossible order.
