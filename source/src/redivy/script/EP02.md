# EP02 — The Price of Staying

## 장면 1 · 클럽 사무실 / 아침
NARRATION: Chloe has called everyone to the club office. Your name is still on last night’s guest list. That makes you a witness, whether you wanted to be one or not.
CHLOE: It’s a photograph. One angle. Half a second.
OLIVER: Was it a kiss?
CHLOE: You’re letting strangers tell you what happened.
OLIVER: I’m asking you.
ETHAN: Yes. It was.
[클로이는 이든을 돌아본다.]
CHLOE: You don’t get to speak for me.
ETHAN: Then stop making me part of a lie.
SOPHIE: Oliver, maybe we should talk outside.
OLIVER: In a minute.
JULIAN: Whoever took it stood by the lounge window. That narrows things down.
LUNA: So does asking who benefits.
CHLOE: And there it is. The new girl has a theory.
[사용자에게]
CHLOE: You were watching the door. Did you see anyone with a phone?

### 선택 C01 · 모르는 것을 인정하기
A. 확인하지 못한 사람을 지목할 수 없다고 말한다.
B. 사람들이 오갔지만 촬영 장면은 보지 못했다고 말한다.
A → CHLOE: Convenient.
A → ETHAN: Accurate. Try it.
B → JULIAN: Good distinction. A phone isn’t a confession.
B → OLIVER: At least one person here can answer a question.
[합류]
OLIVER: We’ll talk later, Chloe. Without an audience.

## 장면 2 · 도서관 / 늦은 저녁
NARRATION: You’re meeting Sophie for a study session. Her laptop has crashed. Chloe arrives before she can recover her paper.
SOPHIE: It’s due at midnight. My scholarship review is next week.
CHLOE: I have a copy of last year’s top paper.
SOPHIE: That isn’t my paper.
CHLOE: It’s a starting point.
SOPHIE: What do you want?
CHLOE: Stay close to Oliver. Keep him occupied. Find out what Luna knows.
SOPHIE: Ask her yourself.
CHLOE: Does your roommate know you still have the messages about taking your exam for you?
[소피가 노트북을 닫는다.]
SOPHIE: Keep your voice down.
CHLOE: Then give me a reason to.
[클로이는 자료가 담긴 봉투를 놓고 떠난다. 소피가 사용자를 본다.]
SOPHIE: You heard enough. Go ahead. Tell me I’m pathetic.

### 선택 C02 · 판단하지 않고 대응하기
A. 협박을 받아들일 필요는 없다고 말한다.
B. 지금 가장 도움이 되는 일이 무엇인지 묻는다.
A → SOPHIE: She isn’t making up the exam. That’s the problem.
A → SOPHIE: I made one awful decision. She’s trying to make it a subscription.
B → SOPHIE: Sit here while I email my professor.
B → SOPHIE: I need one thing tonight that isn’t another lie.
[합류]
SOPHIE: I’m not using that paper. Whatever happens next, that part is mine.

## 장면 3 · 사진관 복도
NARRATION: Luna asks you to come with her to Ethan’s campus darkroom. He photographed last year’s club events.
LUNA: He knew Elena. I want to hear him say it.
[이든이 문을 연다.]
ETHAN: The darkroom’s closed.
LUNA: Then we can talk without an audience.
ETHAN: You brought one.
LUNA: I brought someone who doesn’t owe this club anything.
[루나가 게시판 속 엘레나 사진을 발견한다.]
LUNA: Where did you get that?
ETHAN: I took it.
LUNA: She hated that jacket. She only wore it when she was nervous.
ETHAN: How would you know?
LUNA: She was my sister.
[침묵.]
ETHAN: Luna—
LUNA: Don’t. Not until you tell me what you know.

### 선택 C03 · 대화를 이어갈 방법
A. 이든에게 처음부터 차분히 설명해 달라고 한다.
B. 루나에게 잠깐 멈추고 싶은지 확인한다.
A → ETHAN: Not in the hallway. Come inside.
A → LUNA: And don’t leave anything out to protect me.
B → LUNA: No. I’ve spent a year waiting for someone to answer.
B → ETHAN: Then you deserve an answer.
[합류]
ETHAN: I saw someone with her on the roof. I couldn’t see his face.
LUNA: And you said nothing?
ETHAN: I gave a statement. It disappeared from the file.

## 장면 4 · 암실
[문밖에서 잠금 소리. 이든이 문손잡이를 확인한다.]
ETHAN: Someone’s set the corridor lock.
LUNA: Can you open it from here?
ETHAN: Normally. Not tonight.
[통신은 가능하지만 건물 출입 담당자가 다른 구역에 있다.]
ETHAN: I’m calling campus security. We’re not waiting for whoever did this to change their mind.
[루나는 문을 응시한다.]
LUNA: They know why I’m here.

### 선택 C04 · 루나를 안정시키기
A. 혼자가 아니며 함께 있겠다고 말한다.
B. 지금 아는 사실과 추측을 나눠 정리하자고 한다.
A → LUNA: Then stay where I can see you. Both of you.
A → ETHAN: We’re here.
B → LUNA: Fact: someone locked the door. Guess: they know about Elena.
B → ETHAN: Good. One problem at a time.
[합류]
[관객 전용: 복도 반대편에서 클로이가 방문자용 출입 카드를 주머니에 넣는다.]
CHLOE: Pick up, Ethan.
[이든의 휴대폰에 클로이의 전화가 뜬다.]
END CARD: The door isn’t the only thing keeping them trapped.
