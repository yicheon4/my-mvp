# EP09 — What Honesty Costs

## 장면 1 · 산장 / 새벽
NARRATION: Responders reach the house before dawn. Marcus is taken into custody. The group gives separate accounts. The recordings and files are evidence to examine, not an instant verdict.
DETECTIVE: Tell me what you personally saw and heard. If someone told you something, say who.
[사용자 진술은 선택과 무관하게 전체 사건 기록에 포함된다.]

### 선택 C01 · 진술의 정확성
A. 자신이 직접 들은 마커스의 발언부터 말하겠다고 한다.
B. 시간 순서대로 설명하고 모르는 부분은 구분하겠다고 한다.
A → DETECTIVE: Take your time. Use his words where you remember them.
A → DETECTIVE: It’s okay to say you don’t remember exactly.
B → DETECTIVE: That helps. We can return to details afterward.
B → DETECTIVE: Don’t fill a gap just because it feels unfinished.
[합류]
[밖에서 올리버가 형이 떠나는 모습을 지켜본다.]
OLIVER: I thought I’d feel relieved.
LUNA: Do you?
OLIVER: A little. Then I feel guilty for that, too.
LUNA: Feel whatever you feel. Just don’t let it write your statement.
OLIVER: I won’t.

## 장면 2 · 캠퍼스 상담실 대기 공간 / 며칠 뒤
NARRATION: The university pauses its case against Luna while reviewing the new evidence. Sophie faces a separate inquiry. You wait with her outside an appointment.
SOPHIE: My scholarship is under review. So is my place here.
JULIAN: I’ll wait until you’re done.
SOPHIE: You can’t joke the committee into liking me.
JULIAN: I was thinking more about carrying your bag.
SOPHIE: Oh.
JULIAN: I contain multitudes. Most of them are useless.
[소피가 짧게 웃고 사용자에게 말한다.]
SOPHIE: I finally told the truth. I hate that part of me expected a prize.

### 선택 C02 · 책임과 위로 함께 전하기
A. 책임을 지는 동안에도 도움을 받을 수 있다고 말한다.
B. 결과가 어렵더라도 사실을 바로잡은 선택은 의미 있다고 말한다.
A → SOPHIE: Then wait with me. I don’t need you to say I did nothing wrong.
A → JULIAN: Good. That would be a very long argument.
B → SOPHIE: I’d like to believe that without using it as an excuse.
B → JULIAN: We can remind you of both parts.
[합류]
[소피가 상담실로 들어간다.]

## 장면 3 · 작은 기자 간담회 대기실
NARRATION: Chloe has agreed to give a public statement with her lawyer present. Her phone displays her father’s name. She lets it ring.
CHLOE: He says I’m destroying the family.
ETHAN: What do you say?
CHLOE: Usually? Whatever makes him stop.
CHLOE: I wrote this six times. Every version sounded like someone apologizing for bad weather.
[사용자에게 원고를 건넨다.]
CHLOE: “Mistakes were made.” Listen to that. Nobody even has to exist in that sentence.

### 선택 C03 · 구체적인 사과 돕기
A. 자신이 한 행동을 주어로 분명하게 말하라고 한다.
B. 피해자에게 용서를 요구하는 문장은 빼라고 한다.
A → CHLOE: “I helped conceal evidence. I tried to frame Luna.”
A → CHLOE: That’s harder to hide inside.
B → CHLOE: You’re right. They don’t owe me a clean ending.
B → CHLOE: I can tell the truth without asking for anything back.
[합류]
[기자들 앞.]
CHLOE: My father’s office helped conceal evidence about Elena’s death. I knew about the agreement and kept it hidden.
CHLOE: I also tried to frame Luna for taking exam materials. I have provided my messages and a full statement to the investigators.
CHLOE: I’m not asking anyone to forgive me today.

## 장면 4 · 캠퍼스 벤치 / 학기 말

A. 진실이 밝혀져도 상실이 사라지는 것은 아니라고 말한다.
B. 지금 필요한 것이 무엇인지 묻는다.
A → LUNA: Exactly. She’s still gone. I don’t need that turned into a lesson.
A → LUNA: Thank you for not trying.
B → LUNA: Dinner. Something ordinary. And no speeches.
B → LUNA: Can you manage that?
