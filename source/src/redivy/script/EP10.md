# EP10 — After the Ivy

## 장면 1 · 이든의 사진전 / 1년 뒤
NARRATION: One year later, Ethan opens a small exhibition near campus. You’ve been invited to the opening. At the entrance, a photograph of Elena sits beside a statement approved by her family.
LUNA: She would’ve hated that frame.
ETHAN: You picked it.
LUNA: Sisters are complicated.
[이든이 루나를 바라본다.]
ETHAN: You okay with people asking about her?
LUNA: Tonight, yes. Tomorrow, maybe not.
ETHAN: Then tomorrow we do something else.
[루나가 그의 손을 잡는다. 두 사람은 이제 연인이다.]
LUNA: We’re not good at normal yet.

### 선택 C01 · 재회의 첫마디
A. 억지로 평범해질 필요는 없다고 말한다.
B. 두 사람이 함께 정한 방식이 좋아 보인다고 말한다.
A → LUNA: Good. I was worried there’d be a test.
A → ETHAN: She’d argue with the questions.
B → ETHAN: Mostly she tells me when to stop trying to fix things.
B → LUNA: He’s learning. Slowly.
[합류]
[소피와 줄리언이 들어온다.]
SOPHIE: I’m late. My adviser wanted another draft of my application.
JULIAN: She also wanted lunch. Sophie considered that optional.
SOPHIE: My academic record has enough complications without you reporting my sandwiches.

## 장면 2 · 전시장 복도
NARRATION: Sophie completed her university sanctions and returned to finish her degree. She is preparing a law school application. Julian is completing medical school applications. Neither path has been magically cleared.
JULIAN: These are for you.
[줄리언이 소피에게 작은 꽃다발을 건넨다.]
SOPHIE: Should I check whether Chloe’s standing behind me?
JULIAN: Deserved. No. I know who I’m talking to this time.
JULIAN: Dinner. Saturday. With you. Not as an apology, and not because you need rescuing.
SOPHIE: That was almost a normal invitation.
JULIAN: I practiced on three houseplants.
[소피가 사용자에게 장난스럽게 묻는다.]
SOPHIE: Does he get points for using my actual name?

### 선택 C02 · 어색한 설렘에 반응하기
A. 점수보다 본인이 가고 싶은지가 중요하다고 말한다.
B. 이번에는 꽤 분명하게 물어봤다고 장난스럽게 말한다.
A → SOPHIE: I do. Saturday works.
A → JULIAN: Excellent. The plants were optimistic.
B → SOPHIE: Shockingly clear. Fine. Dinner.
B → JULIAN: I’ll try not to ruin it with a follow-up presentation.
[합류]
SOPHIE: One dinner, Julian.
JULIAN: One dinner. I heard you.

## 장면 3 · 전시장 밖 계단
NARRATION: Chloe waits outside in a plain coat. Her court-ordered obligations are still part of her life. Oliver arrives carrying two coffees. Their engagement remains over.
OLIVER: I remembered how you take it.
CHLOE: I changed it.
OLIVER: Of course you did.
CHLOE: Less sugar. Fewer things I pretend to like.
[사용자가 문을 열어 준다. 클로이는 주저한다.]
CHLOE: Luna said I could come. She didn’t say she wanted to see me.

### 선택 C03 · 화해를 강요하지 않기
A. 초대받은 만큼만 다가가고 상대의 속도를 존중하라고 한다.
B. 변명 없이 짧게 인사하면 된다고 말한다.
A → CHLOE: No grand gesture. No making her manage my feelings.
A → OLIVER: We can leave whenever you need to.
B → CHLOE: “Thank you for inviting me.” I can do that.
B → OLIVER: It’s a good sentence.
[합류]
CHLOE: Oliver. I don’t want to become the old version of us because it’s familiar.
OLIVER: Neither do I. Coffee isn’t an engagement.
CHLOE: You should put that on the cup.
[둘은 웃는다. 재약혼이나 즉각적인 연애 재개로 확정하지 않는다.]

## 장면 4 · 전시장 / 폐관 직전
NARRATION: The seven of you gather around a small table. Marcus has been convicted in Elena’s death. The cover-up led to separate proceedings, including against Chloe and her father. Nobody’s consequences vanished with the final headline.
LUNA: Before we eat. Thank you for coming.
CHLOE: Thank you for letting me.
LUNA: We’re not pretending it never happened.
CHLOE: I know.
SOPHIE: No speeches about how it made us stronger?
LUNA: Absolutely not.
JULIAN: There goes my opening paragraph.
ETHAN: We’re all devastated.
[루나가 사용자 쪽으로 잔을 든다.]
LUNA: You stayed when this wasn’t your mess. Anything you want to say?

### 선택 C04 · 시즌의 마지막 말
A. 앞으로는 서로에게 사실을 말할 수 있기를 바란다고 한다.
B. 과거를 지우지 않으면서 새로운 시간을 쌓아가자고 한다.
A → OLIVER: Even when the truth makes us look bad.
A → CHLOE: Especially then.
B → SOPHIE: New memories. Not replacement ones.
B → LUNA: I can live with that.
[합류]
[모든 휴대폰이 울린다. 익명 메시지.]
UNKNOWN: Think Ivy’s done with you? Remember the other night.
[잠깐의 침묵.]
JULIAN: That’s not me. I feel I should say that immediately.
ETHAN: Nobody deletes anything.
LUNA: Nobody answers alone.
[루나가 휴대폰을 내려놓고 나머지 사람들을 본다.]
LUNA: We send it to the investigator. Then we eat.
[사용자를 포함한 모두가 고개를 끄덕인다.]
NARRATION: Elena’s story has an answer. Whatever this message means, it doesn’t get to rewrite it.
