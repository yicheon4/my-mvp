# EP08 — Dinner with Marcus

## 장면 1 · 산장 거실 / 정전
NARRATION: Battery lamps light the table. Marcus stands between the group and the front door. Outside, the wind has swallowed the road.
MARCUS: Sophie. A scholarship built on someone else taking your exam.
SOPHIE: I’ve already reported it.
MARCUS: Julian. All those favors for club members.
JULIAN: Put them on the list. There’s a list now.
MARCUS: Chloe. Does your father know you borrowed his paperwork?
CHLOE: He can call me himself.
MARCUS: You all think a few files make you untouchable. This road won’t open until morning.
OLIVER: Stop threatening them.
MARCUS: I’m explaining the weather.
[마커스가 사용자에게 시선을 돌린다.]
MARCUS: You don’t belong in this. Walk away when the road opens. Forget their names.

### 선택 C01 · 압박에 답하기
A. 혼자 떠나지 않고 다른 사람들과 함께 있겠다고 말한다.
B. 누구도 다치지 않게 차분히 이야기하자고 말한다.
A → MARCUS: Loyalty. Expensive habit.
A → OLIVER: Then let me pay for once.
B → MARCUS: That depends on how reasonable everyone is.
B → LUNA: Asking for safety isn’t being unreasonable.
[합류]
[마커스는 출입구를 계속 막는다. 위협은 대사와 위치로만 표현한다.]

## 장면 2 · 거실과 지하 계단
CHLOE: If you want the original, you need to know which copy you’re holding.
MARCUS: Don’t try to be clever.
CHLOE: Then stop giving me opportunities.
[클로이가 서류를 펼쳐 시선을 끈다. 줄리언과 이든은 조명을 확인하겠다며 계단 쪽으로 간다. 사용자는 소피 옆에 남는다.]
SOPHIE: The router’s down. The lawyer knows to call for help if we miss the check-in.
SOPHIE: I’m recording what he says. The old files are already somewhere safe.
[녹음은 극중 소품 연출이며 앱이 사용자 주변 소리를 몰래 녹음하지 않는다.]
LUNA: I can’t listen to him say her name like she’s a problem he solved.

### 선택 C02 · 루나를 지지하기
A. 지금 답하지 않아도 되며 곁에 있겠다고 한다.
B. 잠깐 자신을 보며 호흡을 가다듬자고 말한다.
A → LUNA: Stay. Don’t ask me to be calm. Just stay.
A → SOPHIE: We’re right here.
B → LUNA: Okay. I’m here. I can hear you.
B → SOPHIE: You don’t have to do this alone.
[합류]
[관객 전용: 지하에서 이든과 줄리언이 백업 전원을 확인한다.]
ETHAN: Your family owns a mountain house and buys the cheapest backup battery?
JULIAN: Save the review. Hold the light.
[표준 전원 복구만 연출. 구체적인 전기 작업 절차는 없음.]

## 장면 3 · 식탁
CHLOE: My father paid to bury a camera record. Why would he pay if Elena simply fell?
MARCUS: Because she was going to ruin people over an initiation prank.
LUNA: She was going to report you.
MARCUS: She had no idea what she was threatening.
LUNA: So you threatened her back.
MARCUS: I grabbed her. She pulled away. I pushed her. I didn’t think she’d go over.
[긴 침묵.]
OLIVER: You told me you never touched her.
MARCUS: And you believed me because it was easier.
OLIVER: Yes. I did.
MARCUS: Don’t act brave now. You’ll say what I tell you, like last time.
[올리버가 사용자 쪽을 본다.]

### 선택 C03 · 올리버의 결심 지지
A. 이번에는 스스로 사실을 말할 수 있다고 한다.
B. 형의 행동까지 대신 책임질 필요는 없다고 한다.
A → OLIVER: I heard you, Marcus. All of us did.
A → OLIVER: I’m not changing it for you.
B → OLIVER: I’ll answer for my lie. You answer for what you did to Elena.
B → MARCUS: You ungrateful little—
[합류]
OLIVER: No. You don’t get to finish that sentence.

## 장면 4 · 통신 복구
NARRATION: The router lights return. Julian reaches the lawyer, who has already alerted emergency services after the missed check-in. Sophie sends the new recording to the lawyer.
JULIAN: They have our location. They know who’s here.
[마커스가 녹음기를 향해 움직인다. 올리버가 물러서지 않는다.]
OLIVER: Leave it. Taking it won’t bring the files back.
[사용자는 연결된 전화에 짧게 상황을 알린다.]

### 선택 C04 · 도움을 요청하는 핵심 전달
A. 일곱 명의 일행이 함께 있으며 위협받고 있다고 알린다.
B. 출입구가 막혀 있으며 안전한 곳으로 이동하는 안내가 필요하다고 알린다.
A → OPERATOR: Stay on the line. Help is on the way.
A → JULIAN: Everyone’s accounted for.
B → OPERATOR: Don’t confront him. Stay on the line and follow my instructions.
B → ETHAN: We’re staying together.
[합류]
NARRATION: Far below the house, lights move through the snow. Marcus looks toward the window. For the first time tonight, he has nothing to say.
END CARD: The silence belongs to him now.
