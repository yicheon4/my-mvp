# EP05 — No More Favors

## 장면 1 · 클럽 사무실
NARRATION: Luna’s case is under review. Oliver has asked you to return the party files. Inside the office, Chloe places her engagement ring on the desk—then puts it back on.
CHLOE: You embarrassed me in front of the dean.
OLIVER: You put someone in front of the dean.
CHLOE: You don’t know that.
OLIVER: I know what I saw when you thought nobody was watching.
CHLOE: Is this about Sophie?
OLIVER: I kissed her. I should have ended this first. That part is on me.
CHLOE: How honest of you. Now that it’s convenient.
OLIVER: You’re right. It’s late. It’s still over.
CHLOE: Our families won’t let you walk away.
OLIVER: They can cancel a party. They can’t make me marry you.
[클로이가 반지를 내려놓는다. 올리버는 사용자에게 돌아선다.]
OLIVER: Tell me that didn’t sound rehearsed.

### 선택 C01 · 이별 후 현실적인 반응
A. 말한 경계를 앞으로도 지켜야 한다고 한다.
B. 소피에게도 사실을 직접 설명하라고 한다.
A → OLIVER: I know. Saying it was the easy part.
A → CHLOE: Enjoy it while it lasts.
B → OLIVER: I will. Without making her responsible for this.
B → CHLOE: That would be a first.
[합류]
OLIVER: I’m sorry I waited this long. I’m not sorry it’s ending.

## 장면 2 · 옥상 출입구 / 낮
NARRATION: Sophie asks you to stay nearby while she meets Chloe. She keeps the door open.
CHLOE: You wore my dress. You took my fiancé. Anything else you’d like?
SOPHIE: My name back. You’ve been using it like an employee badge.
CHLOE: You think you’re better than me?
SOPHIE: No. I think I helped you hurt someone.
[클로이가 소피의 손목을 붙잡는다. 소피가 손을 뺀다.]
SOPHIE: Don’t touch me.
CHLOE: You have no idea what my father can do.
SOPHIE: I kept your messages. And the voice note telling me where to bring Luna.
CHLOE: You recorded me?
SOPHIE: You sent it to me. You assumed I’d keep protecting you.

### 선택 C02 · 상황을 진정시키기
A. 더 가까이 오지 말고 거리를 두라고 한다.
B. 대화를 끝내고 소피와 함께 나가겠다고 한다.
A → CHLOE: I’m not going to hurt her.
A → SOPHIE: Then step back.
B → CHLOE: Running away?
B → SOPHIE: Leaving. You keep confusing the two.
[합류]
SOPHIE: I’m sending it to the investigator. Not trading it for another favor.

## 장면 3 · 도서관 스터디룸
NARRATION: Sophie brings the messages to Luna and Ethan. You join them to review what happened, not to decide anyone’s guilt.
SOPHIE: Chloe gave me the envelope. I left it in Luna’s bag while she was outside.
LUNA: You watched me open it.
SOPHIE: Yes.
LUNA: And you let me think I was losing my mind.
SOPHIE: Yes.
ETHAN: Sophie—
LUNA: Let her answer.
SOPHIE: I’m not asking you to forgive me. The messages back up what I’m telling you.
LUNA: Why now?
SOPHIE: Because I saw your face. And I recognized what I was becoming.

### 선택 C03 · 동맹의 조건
A. 증거를 받는 것과 용서하는 것은 별개라고 말한다.
B. 소피가 자신의 행동도 조사관에게 진술해야 한다고 말한다.
A → LUNA: Exactly. I’ll take the evidence. The rest isn’t available tonight.
A → SOPHIE: Understood.
B → SOPHIE: I already requested a meeting. You can read the email.
B → LUNA: Keep that promise when it stops feeling brave.
[합류]
ETHAN: We keep the originals. No edits. No public guessing games.

## 장면 4 · 스터디룸 밖
LUNA: Every time someone helps, I find out what they did first.
LUNA: How am I supposed to trust any of them?

### 선택 C04 · 신뢰의 기준
A. 당장 믿지 않아도 되며 행동을 지켜보자고 한다.
B. 앞으로 지켜야 할 약속을 구체적으로 정하자고 한다.
A → LUNA: Actions. Not the version they tell about themselves.
A → ETHAN: Including mine.
B → LUNA: No secrets about Elena. No posting evidence for attention.
B → SOPHIE: And no asking you to make us feel better.
[합류]
[관객 전용: 클로이가 혼자 아버지에게 전화한다.]
