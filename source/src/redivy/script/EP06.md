# EP06 — The Man in the Coat

## 장면 1 · 줄리언의 방
NARRATION: Julian has agreed to talk. Luna brings you and Ethan. A hard drive sits on the desk beside an untouched drink.
JULIAN: I took the terrace photo. I posted it. I sent you the anonymous message, too.
ETHAN: Why?
JULIAN: I wanted Chloe to stop lying. I wanted her to look at me. Neither answer gets prettier out loud.
LUNA: You used a scandal to point people toward my sister?
JULIAN: I was afraid of putting my name on what I knew.
ETHAN: And now?
JULIAN: Now someone tried to get Luna expelled. I ran out of excuses.
[줄리언이 드라이브를 가리킨다.]
JULIAN: Event footage. Last year. I kept a copy before they cleared the club archive.

### 선택 C01 · 폭로자의 책임 확인
A. 지금까지 왜 증거를 숨겼는지 끝까지 설명하라고 한다.
B. 파일이 무엇을 보여주는지 먼저 확인하자고 한다.
A → JULIAN: Marcus threatened to expose the cheating I helped cover up. I protected my future and called it protecting my friends.
A → LUNA: Don’t call it that again.
B → JULIAN: A timestamp. The roof landing. Marcus wearing Oliver’s coat.
B → ETHAN: Show us the whole clip. Not just the frame that fits.
[합류]
NARRATION: The footage shows Marcus following Elena upstairs. The fall itself is out of frame. A crash is heard. Marcus returns alone.

## 장면 2 · 같은 방 / 올리버 도착
OLIVER: I knew this would come out.
LUNA: Did you see him push her?
OLIVER: No. He told me she fell. He told me he never touched her.
ETHAN: You said you were upstairs.
OLIVER: I said I was the one wearing the coat. That Marcus had left. I gave him an alibi.
LUNA: You made yourself the person everyone suspected.
OLIVER: My father said Marcus had a career. I had time to recover.
LUNA: Elena didn’t.
[올리버는 고개를 숙인다.]
OLIVER: I know. There’s no answer that makes that better.

### 선택 C02 · 증언을 분명하게 만들기
A. 형에게 직접 들은 말과 가족에게 들은 말을 구분해 달라고 한다.
B. 조사관에게 기존 진술을 바로잡을 의향이 있는지 묻는다.
A → OLIVER: Marcus said she fell. My father told me what to say afterward. I’ll write both down separately.
A → LUNA: Use their words. Don’t soften them.
B → OLIVER: Yes. With a lawyer. I won’t make another version for my family first.
B → ETHAN: Then make the appointment now.
[합류]
JULIAN: There’s more. The payment agreement names Chloe’s father’s office. I have a scan. Chloe has the original.

## 장면 3 · 기숙사로 가는 길
NARRATION: Copies of the files have been sent to Luna’s lawyer. Ethan walks ahead to collect her remaining papers from her room. You stay with Luna outside.
LUNA: For a year, I thought Oliver was the man in the coat.
LUNA: I wanted it to be simple. A name. A face. Someone I could hate without doing any more work.

### 선택 C03 · 복잡해진 진실 받아들이기
A. 잘못 짐작한 부분을 인정해도 진실을 찾는 이유는 바뀌지 않는다고 말한다.
B. 지금 결론을 내리지 말고 확인된 사실부터 정리하자고 한다.
A → LUNA: Elena still deserves the truth. Even when it isn’t the truth I prepared for.
A → LUNA: That’s harder than I expected.
B → LUNA: Marcus was there. Oliver lied. The fall is off camera.
B → LUNA: We still need to know what happened between those moments.
[합류]
[건물 안에서 물건이 떨어지는 소리. 이든이 문 근처에 쓰러져 있고, 낯선 두 사람이 반대 출구로 달아난다. 공격 장면은 보여주지 않는다.]
LUNA: Ethan? Ethan, look at me!

## 장면 4 · 기숙사 입구
[이든은 의식이 있지만 혼란스러워한다. 사용자는 긴급 신고를 한다.]
OPERATOR: Emergency services. What’s the address?
[주소는 자동으로 전달되는 연출. 발화 연습으로 구조를 지연시키지 않는다.]
LUNA: I shouldn’t have let him go in alone.

### 선택 C04 · 위기 상황에서 안심시키기
A. 도움이 오고 있으며 지금 곁에 있자고 말한다.
B. 루나에게 신고 담당자의 말을 함께 듣자고 한다.
A → LUNA: Okay. Ethan, we’re staying right here.
A → ETHAN: The copies…
B → LUNA: Right. I’m listening. Tell us what you need.
B → ETHAN: Don’t go after them.
[합류]
LUNA: Nobody is. The files are safe. You hear me? They’re safe.
NARRATION: Ethan closes his eyes as the sirens approach.
END CARD: They stole the papers. They didn’t erase the truth.
