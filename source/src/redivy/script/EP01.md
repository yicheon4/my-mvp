# EP01 — A Perfect Party

## 장면 1 · 환영 파티 / 저녁
[배경: 크림슨 홀. 표정: 클로이=여유 있는 미소, 올리버=지친 미소, 소피=관찰, 줄리언=장난.]
NARRATION: St. Jude University. The Crimson Club’s first party of the semester. You’re a new member helping at the welcome desk. Everyone here knows someone. Nobody seems to trust them.
CHLOE: To old friends, new faces, and a year worth remembering.
ETHAN: Ambitious. Most people here are trying to forget the last one.
JULIAN: And that’s why Ethan doesn’t get a microphone.
OLIVER: Can we finish the toast before we start a fight?
CHLOE: Of course, darling. Smile. My father’s office follows the club account.
OLIVER: Does he want a son-in-law or a campaign poster?
CHLOE: Tonight? Good lighting.
SOPHIE: Oliver, have some water.
OLIVER: I’m fine.
SOPHIE: You’ve been holding an empty glass for ten minutes.
JULIAN: She notices everything. It’s comforting until it’s about you.
SOPHIE: Your cuff is in the dip.
JULIAN: Case in point.
[올리버는 잔을 내려놓는다. 클로이는 사용자의 명찰을 읽는다.]
CHLOE: Our new member. Tell me—are you here for the connections or the company?

### 선택 C01 · 첫인상 만들기
A. 사람들을 알아가고 싶다고 편하게 답한다.
B. 들어오기 힘든 모임이라 궁금했다고 솔직히 답한다.
[선택 의도를 사용자가 영어로 발화 → 아래 해당 반응.]
A → CHLOE: How refreshing. Someone who admits they like people.
A → ETHAN: Give it an hour.
B → CHLOE: Exclusivity does get people’s attention.
B → JULIAN: So does a fire alarm. We aim higher.
[합류]
OLIVER: Don’t let them scare you off. They rehearse this.

## 장면 2 · VIP 라운지
NARRATION: Later, the six of them gather in a private lounge. Julian asks you to stay and help with the party game. Luna, a transfer student, takes the last chair.
LUNA: Is this seat taken?
CHLOE: Depends. Were you invited?
JULIAN: By me. Try not to make that sound like a crime.
SOPHIE: The rules are simple. One question. One honest answer.
ETHAN: And absolutely no way to verify either.
CHLOE: Luna. Why did you really leave your last school?
LUNA: I transferred.
CHLOE: People usually have a reason. Bad grades? Someone else’s boyfriend?
LUNA: Do you always offer answers before you ask a question?
CHLOE: Only when the truth needs encouragement.
[루나는 사용자 쪽을 본다.]
LUNA: Is this how they welcomed you, too?

### 선택 C02 · 공격적인 질문에 개입하기
A. 답하고 싶지 않으면 답하지 않아도 된다고 말한다.
B. 클로이에게 왜 그 질문이 중요한지 묻는다.
A → LUNA: Thank you. I can answer. I just wanted to know the rules.
A → CHLOE: Apparently we’re writing new ones.
B → CHLOE: I like to know who’s in my house.
B → LUNA: Then you should ask what brought me here.
[합류]
LUNA: I came to find out why someone died—and why everyone stopped asking about it.
OLIVER: Who?
LUNA: Elena. Last year’s initiation.
[올리버의 미소가 사라진다. 이든은 잔을 내려놓는다.]
SOPHIE: That was investigated.
LUNA: That’s not the same as explained.
JULIAN: Okay. We’re done with the game.
ETHAN: Funny. It was just getting honest.

## 장면 3 · 라운지 바깥 복도
NARRATION: The room empties quickly. Luna stays behind. Through the glass doors, Chloe heads toward the terrace. Ethan follows.
LUNA: Say a name, and suddenly nobody’s thirsty.
LUNA: You saw them. Was I imagining that?

### 선택 C03 · 관찰한 사실 말하기
A. 올리버가 엘레나의 이름에 굳었다고 말한다.
B. 이든이 놀라기보다 이미 알고 있는 것처럼 보였다고 말한다.
A → LUNA: Oliver. Right. I noticed his hands.
A → LUNA: I’ll ask him. I won’t decide what it means yet.
B → LUNA: That’s what I thought. He didn’t ask who she was.
B → LUNA: Knowing her isn’t a crime. Pretending not to might matter.
[합류]
LUNA: Thanks for saying what you actually saw.
[관객 전용 장면. 사용자는 이 대화를 듣지 못한다.]
CHLOE: You enjoyed that.
ETHAN: You mean the part where you cornered a stranger?
CHLOE: You’ve been looking at me all night.
ETHAN: You’ve been checking.
CHLOE: Tell me to leave.
ETHAN: Do you want to?
CHLOE: No.
[두 사람은 잠시 서로를 보고 입을 맞춘다. 이든이 먼저 물러난다.]
ETHAN: What about Oliver?
CHLOE: Don’t say his name right now.
[화면 암전.]

## 장면 4 · 다음 날 / 휴대폰
NARRATION: By morning, an anonymous post on Ivy Talk has reached the whole campus.
IVY TALK: CRIMSON’S GOLDEN COUPLE? ASK CHLOE WHO SHE KISSED ON THE TERRACE.
[사진: 테라스의 클로이와 이든. 노골적인 묘사 없이 실루엣.]
GROUP CHAT — CHLOE: Nobody forwards this. We meet in twenty minutes.
GROUP CHAT — JULIAN: That should calm everyone down.
GROUP CHAT — LUNA: Someone wanted this seen.
PRIVATE MESSAGE — LUNA: Are you coming?

### 선택 C04 · 소문을 대하는 태도
A. 사진을 더 퍼뜨리지 않고 직접 이야기를 듣겠다고 한다.
B. 비난부터 하기 전에 사진 출처를 확인하자고 한다.
A → LUNA: Good. An audience is the last thing they need.
A → LUNA: Meet me outside the club office.
B → LUNA: Exactly. Whoever posted it picked the timing.
B → LUNA: Let’s find out why.
[합류]
PRIVATE MESSAGE — UNKNOWN: You were in that room. Ask them what happened to Elena.
NARRATION: The message disappears before you can reply.
END CARD: Someone is watching. Someone wants you to watch, too.
