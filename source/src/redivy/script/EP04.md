# EP04 — The Wrong Red Dress

## 장면 1 · 가면무도회 / 저녁
NARRATION: Two weeks later, Crimson hosts its masquerade. You’re helping with check-in. Chloe has lent Sophie a red dress. Luna has received an invitation to meet a professor about Elena.
JULIAN: Masks stay on until ten. Regrets are available all evening.
SOPHIE: You sound rehearsed.
JULIAN: It’s a party. Nobody wants the first draft.
[클로이가 소피의 리본을 매만진다.]
CHLOE: Professor Bell’s office. Nine fifteen. Walk Luna there.
SOPHIE: You said we were done after tonight.
CHLOE: Then finish.
[클로이가 떠난다. 줄리언은 붉은 드레스의 소피를 클로이로 착각해 다가온다.]
JULIAN: You can stop pretending with me.
SOPHIE: Pretending what?
JULIAN: That Oliver makes you happy. That Ethan will ever stay.
JULIAN: I’ve been right here, Chloe. This whole time.
[소피가 가면을 벗는다.]
SOPHIE: Wrong audience.
JULIAN: Sophie. God. I’m sorry.
SOPHIE: For saying it, or for saying it to me?
[사용자가 가까이 온다.]

### 선택 C01 · 어색한 고백 뒤 개입
A. 줄리언에게 소피에게 제대로 사과하라고 말한다.
B. 소피에게 잠깐 바람을 쐬러 가겠냐고 묻는다.
A → JULIAN: You’re right. Sophie, I treated you like a costume. You didn’t deserve that.
A → SOPHIE: No. I didn’t.
B → SOPHIE: Yes. Before someone else mistakes me for a better investment.
B → JULIAN: That’s not what I meant. But I heard how it sounded.
[합류]
SOPHIE: I have to find Luna. Something’s wrong.

## 장면 2 · 교수 연구실 복도
NARRATION: Luna is waiting outside Professor Bell’s office. You follow Sophie into the quiet hallway.
LUNA: His office is dark. Are you sure he’s expecting me?
SOPHIE: Don’t go in.
LUNA: You brought me here.
SOPHIE: I know. I’m trying to undo it.
[보안 직원과 학장이 다가온다.]
DEAN: Luna? We received a report about missing examination materials.
LUNA: From whom?
DEAN: We need to ask you some questions.
SOPHIE: She was invited here. By me.
DEAN: We’ll speak to you separately.
[루나는 가방을 열어 보이다 낯선 봉투를 발견한다.]
LUNA: This isn’t mine.
SECURITY: Please leave it where it is.

### 선택 C02 · 성급한 결론 막기
A. 가방에서 나왔다는 것만으로 가져간 사람을 알 수는 없다고 말한다.
B. 현장에서 본 내용을 별도로 진술하겠다고 한다.
A → DEAN: No finding has been made. This will be reviewed.
A → LUNA: Then please speak to me like that’s true.
B → SECURITY: Give us your name. We’ll take your statement separately.
B → SOPHIE: Take mine, too. All of it.
[합류]
CHLOE: Luna? What happened?
LUNA: You tell me.
CHLOE: Careful. Accusations need evidence.

## 장면 3 · 복도 끝
[올리버가 소란을 듣고 온다.]
OLIVER: What time were the papers taken?
DEAN: Between eight forty and nine, according to the initial report.
OLIVER: Luna was with me. Outside, by the courtyard.
CHLOE: Oliver. Think carefully.
OLIVER: I am.
LUNA: We talked about Elena.
OLIVER: And I should have done that sooner.
DEAN: We’ll check the timing. Both of you will need to give statements.
[학장이 이동한다. 루나는 사용자를 향한다.]
LUNA: I thought they were going to decide before I opened my mouth.

### 선택 C03 · 다음 행동 정하기
A. 기억이 흐려지기 전에 시간 순서를 적자고 한다.
B. 조사 절차를 함께 확인해 줄 사람을 찾자고 한다.
A → LUNA: Eight forty-five, courtyard. Just before nine, Sophie’s message.
A → OLIVER: I still have the photo I took of the clock outside. It helps place us there.
B → LUNA: The student adviser. I have her email.
B → OLIVER: I’ll wait while you call. You shouldn’t have to explain this alone.
[합류]
LUNA: I’m not asking anyone to lie for me.
OLIVER: You don’t have to. This time I’m telling the truth.

## 장면 4 · 무도회 입구
[사용자와 소피가 남는다.]
SOPHIE: I knew she was planning something. I told myself I could stop it later.
SOPHIE: Later is a wonderful word when you’re a coward.

### 선택 C04 · 책임 있는 도움 요청
A. 자책보다 알고 있는 사실을 말하는 게 먼저라고 한다.
B. 누구의 지시였는지 분명히 말해 달라고 한다.
A → SOPHIE: Right. I’ll write it down. Even the parts that make me look guilty.
A → SOPHIE: Especially those.
B → SOPHIE: Chloe. She threatened me, but I still agreed to bring Luna here.
B → SOPHIE: I won’t pretend I had no choice.
[합류]
[관객 전용: 클로이와 올리버.]
CHLOE: You chose her over me.
OLIVER: I chose something that actually happened.
CHLOE: Since when does that matter to you?
[올리버가 굳는다.]
END CARD: One true alibi. One old lie.
